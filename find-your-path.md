# Find your path

### I collect samples in my own research

You want **For Researchers**. Start with **Create a SESAR account**.

### I run a lab group

Same section, but the setup order matters and getting it wrong means redoing it. Start with **Share a code with your lab group**.

### I manage a collection, repository, or museum

You want **For Institutions & Repositories** — different scale, different onboarding, and possibly a different registration route.

### I'm building software that talks to SESAR

You want the **API** section, and probably the **Metadata Schema** alongside it.

### I need to know what goes in a field

**Metadata Schema** for what each field means and whether it's required. **Vocabularies** for which values are allowed.

### I'm mapping SESAR to another standard

**Metadata Schema → Crosswalks** covers DataCite, Darwin Core, and iSamples.

### I'm looking for samples, not registering them

Try the [SESAR Catalog](https://www.geosamples.org/search-options/catalog-search) or the [Index to Marine and Lacustrine Geological Samples](https://imlgs.geosamples.org).

### I want to know what SESAR is

That's on [geosamples.org](https://www.geosamples.org/about) rather than here.
