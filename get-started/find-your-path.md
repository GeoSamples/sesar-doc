# Find your path

### I collect samples in my own research

You want [**For Researchers**](https://app.gitbook.com/o/kGnUCKqXIDfoijyR9mJD/s/td44sRg81erEWSwelkyG/). Start with [**Create a SESAR account**](https://app.gitbook.com/s/td44sRg81erEWSwelkyG/accounts-and-access/readme).

### I run a lab group

Same section, but the setup order matters and getting it wrong means redoing it. Start with [**Share a code with your lab group**](https://app.gitbook.com/s/td44sRg81erEWSwelkyG/accounts-and-access/share-a-code-with-your-lab-group).

### I manage a collection, repository, or museum

<mark style="color:pink;">\[COMING SOON]</mark> You want **For Institutions & Repositories** — different scale, different onboarding, and possibly a different registration route.&#x20;

### I'm building software that talks to SESAR

You want the [**API**](https://app.gitbook.com/o/kGnUCKqXIDfoijyR9mJD/s/Ek5ePMNueg0oUxjBjeBl/) section, and probably the [**Metadata Schema**](https://app.gitbook.com/o/kGnUCKqXIDfoijyR9mJD/s/2LSKTZUdmn3ddJ9cBWvW/) alongside it.

### I need to know what goes in a field

[**Metadata Schema**](https://app.gitbook.com/o/kGnUCKqXIDfoijyR9mJD/s/2LSKTZUdmn3ddJ9cBWvW/) for what each field means and whether it's required, and [**Vocabularies**](https://app.gitbook.com/o/kGnUCKqXIDfoijyR9mJD/s/EniiGcb4boFyc9oVYT1O/) describing which values are allowed.

### I'm mapping SESAR to another standard

<mark style="color:pink;">\[COMING SOON]</mark> **Metadata Schema → Crosswalks** covers DataCite, Darwin Core, and iSamples.

### I'm looking for samples, not registering them

Try the [SESAR Catalog](https://www.geosamples.org/search-options/catalog-search) or the [Index to Marine and Lacustrine Geological Samples](https://imlgs.geosamples.org).

### I want to know what SESAR is

That's on [geosamples.org](https://www.geosamples.org/about) rather than here.
