# Updating Sample Metadata

## Individual Sample Update

Individual edits can be made on 'Samples'. Scroll to the 'Edit' button on the right side of your sample list. Update the metadata and then click 'Update sample'.

## Batch (Bulk) Update

Batch update for your samples is accessible by going to your 'Your Samples' or 'Team Samples' page. Navigate to this page under 'Samples' on the left hand panel.&#x20;

1. Select the samples that you want to batch update.
2. Click the 'Bulk Update' tool on top of the page.
3. On the 'Bulk update samples' popup, choose a field to update under 'Field to update'.
4. The left side, 'Before' will show the current metadata associated with the field you want to update. The right side, 'After' presents two options, the first to update the values and the other to clear any current values.
5. Under 'After', input the new value.
   1. Most fields will be free text. Certain fields such as 'Object type', 'General material type', and 'Material types' will be a drop down menu.&#x20;
   2. Other fields such as 'Age (min/ma/unit)' will have multiple fields. For 'Collector(s)', 'Launch platform', 'Cruise/field program' and 'Original archive', you will need to add an existing value pre-saved during registration or be asked to add a new 'collector', 'launch', 'archive' etc.&#x20;
6. Click ‘Update x samples’ (x represents the number of samples you choose to update).
7. Confirm your update is successful by reviewing your samples.

You can also bulk update samples for 'Sample Groups' by accessing the group you created and following the same process listed above.&#x20;

### Updating Initiative, Platform, and Launch Platform

1. Navigate to 'Your Samples'.
2. Click the samples you wish to add 'Initiative', 'Platform', and 'Launch Platform'.
3. Click 'Bulk Update'.
4. Select 'Field to update' and find 'Cruise/field program'.
5. Select 'Add cruise/field program' which is your specific 'Initiative' or 'Clear field instead' if you want to remove the current 'Initiative' instead.
6. 'Add new initiative' or choose an existing one and click 'Done'.
7. If you added the wrong one, select 'Clear selection' and try again.
8. Click 'Update X samples' (x is the number of samples you selected) when you are finished to save your changes
