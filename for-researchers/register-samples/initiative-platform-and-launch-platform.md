# Initiative, Platform, and Launch Platform

Initiative, Platform, and Launch Platforms can now be added as separate entries in MySESAR. You can add, edit, and delete\* them, and link them to your samples during registration. A maximum of 10 entries are possible for each metadata field per registration.

\* Only initiatives, platforms, and launch platforms that are not linked to a sample can be deleted. &#x20;

### Adding New Initiative, Platform, and Launch Platform

The process for adding them in MySESAR is the same. You can access them on the left hand panel under 'Your Samples'/'Team Samples' or by adding them during the time of your registrations. Please follow this guide:

1. If you are using the left hand panel, choose which of the initiative, platform, and launch platforms you want to add. Click the '+ Add'.
2. Enter the name and related information. Name is required, however, other metadata fields are not.&#x20;
3. After adding, the initiative, platform, or launch platform should be added and updated on the page.
4. Using the three dots on the right hand side, you can 'View Details & Samples', 'Edit', and 'Delete'.

### Linking Initiative, Platform, and Launch Platform to Samples

After adding Initiative, Platform, and Launch Platform, you can add them to your sample metadata during and after registration.

#### Adding After Sample Registration - Edit Sample

1. Navigate to 'Edit' an individual sample under 'Samples'.
2. Under 'Sampling method' tab, scroll down to 'Cruise/field program’'to 'Add Initiative'.
3. You can choose the initiative from your save 'Initiatives' or add a new one. New additions will be added to your list of 'Initiatives'.
4. Repeat this process for Platform and Launch platforms.&#x20;
5. Click 'Update Sample'.
