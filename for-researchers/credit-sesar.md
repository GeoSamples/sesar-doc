# Crediting SESAR

Please, acknowledge SESAR as the metadata repository for your samples in your FAIR and accessible data statement or the acknowledgements of your publication. An example statement that credits SESAR is as follows:

“To ensure the accessibility and discoverability of the samples used in this research, and to align with the National Science Foundation’s guidelines of effective data practices, all samples used in this study have been registered with International Generic Sample Numbers (IGSNs) through the System for Earth and Extraterrestrial Sample Registration (SESAR). SESAR is maintained by the GeoInformatics Research Group of the Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory. [http://geosamples.org/](http://geosamples.org/) Accessed DAY MONTH YEAR”
